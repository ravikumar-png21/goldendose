How the medicine meter works

STEP 1

You count once

Count the tablets in hand and type the number. Say how many make one dose: 1, or 0.5 for half a tablet.

STEP 2

It counts down by itself

Every dose time that passes takes one dose away, even if Kamala forgot to tap Taken. No daily typing.

STEP 3

Running low at 5 days

With about 5 days left, a Running low box appears on Kamala's page and every family member gets one message.

STEP 4

Order, then count again

Order on WhatsApp writes a one-month order to your chemist. When the new strip comes, count again: the meter starts fresh.

Example: Telma 40, one tablet at 8 am and 8 pm (2 a day)
