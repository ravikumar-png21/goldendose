My preparation strategy · GoldenDose demo

Learn every click. Then repeat it live.

The click-by-click slides are my script. In the demo, I do exactly what each slide shows.

Before the day · how I prepare

1

Study each slide deeply

For every click: where to tap, why we tap it, and what should appear next.

2

Practise side by side

Slides on one half of the screen, the app on the other. Do each click as I read it.

3

Rehearse the full run

All 50 clicks, start to finish, with a real alarm ringing. Repeat until it feels easy.
