Kamala never sees "missed" in red. A late dose says "Not taken yet" in calm brown, and the family is told instead.

GoldenDose · 6

"Words like 'big' and 'clear' can't be tested, so each one became a number. The typeface is Atkinson Hyperlegible, made by the Braille Institute for low vision: that's why the zero has a slash, so it's never confused with the letter O." The bottom line is an emotional design choice: avoid worrying the elder, but make sure the family can act.
