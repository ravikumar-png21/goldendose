Field notes

Six real needs, from watching an older person

Hard to read

Twice-size text, high contrast, read aloud, a photo of the pill

Wrong taps

Few, huge buttons. Quick double taps ignored. Undo for 5 minutes

Missed reminders

Top priority: a loud full-screen alarm that repeats until answered

Getting lost

One screen: today's medicines in time order. Setup lives with the family

"Did I take it?"

A big green tick with the time: "Taken at 8:15 am"

Calling for help

Family is messaged if a dose is 30 minutes late
