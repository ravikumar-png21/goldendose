How it works

Three simple steps

1

The family sets it up

Add each medicine, its time, and a photo of the pill. Done once.

2

The alarm reminds her

The screen fills, shows the pill and reads it aloud. She taps "Taken".

3

The family is told

If a dose is 30 minutes late, they get a message by SMS, WhatsApp or app.

GoldenDose · 7

That's the whole idea. Setup is done once by the family; every day after, the older person only needs one tap.
