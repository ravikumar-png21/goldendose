The idea behind it

Simple for the elder.
Peace of mind for the family.

One screen

Nothing to get lost in.

Big and clear

Large text, huge buttons.

It comes to her

The alarm finds her.

Mistakes are cheap

Undo makes it safe to tap.

Never frightening

Calm words, no red warnings.

Family has her back

Told if something is missed.

Every design decision comes back to these six ideas. For example, a late dose says "Not taken yet" in calm brown on her screen; only the family sees "Missed".
