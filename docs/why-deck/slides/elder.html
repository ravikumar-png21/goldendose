Benefits for the older person

Easy enough to use every day

Never misses a dose

A loud alarm that repeats until she answers.

Takes the right pill

She sees a picture of it, and hears its name.

No more "did I take it?"

A big green tick and the time, at a glance.

Nothing to be afraid of

Big text, one screen, no password, and Undo for mistakes.

It also keeps working without internet: the reminders still ring, and her taps are sent when the connection returns.
