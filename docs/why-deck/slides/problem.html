Why we built it

Many older people take several medicines a day. Remembering them all is hard.

They forget

A small phone reminder is easy to miss, or to swipe away by mistake.

They mix them up

Many pills look alike, and medicine names are hard to read and remember.

They aren't sure

"Did I already take it?" So they skip a dose, or take it twice.

GoldenDose · 2

Keep it human. Most of us know a parent or grandparent like this. Taking medicines correctly every day matters for their health, but today it depends on memory.
